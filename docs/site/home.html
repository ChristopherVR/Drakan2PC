Welcome to Drakan2PC

Return to the world of Rynn and Arokh with a native PC port of Drakan: The Ancients' Gates.

Built from your own PlayStation 2 disc, with higher frame rates, proper mouse and keyboard controls, and optional graphics and gameplay improvements.

You will need your own PAL disc image.
Supported release: SCES-500.06. No game files are included.

Make it your own

60 FPS and beyond
Choose 30, 60 or uncapped world frame rates. Performance depends on your hardware.
PC controls and combat
Direct mouse look, rebindable keys, crosshair bow aiming and optional dual wielding.
A configurable interface
Modern Tab inventory, an M map, an I inventory strip and full PC settings.
Enhanced graphics and movies
Optional HD textures, upscaled video, narration subtitles and supported DLSS / ray-traced effects.

Prefer the original style? Keep 30 FPS, classic bow controls and the original menus, and disable optional effects. Windows currently has the fullest feature set.
